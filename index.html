About

For a generation asking, “Which way now?”

Two days centered on the gospel, your spiritual life, and your life choices.

Who comes to COMPASS

Unmarried college students and young professionals from Chinese churches in Kansas, Missouri, and Nebraska.

COMPASS is for Christians seeking to find God’s direction in:

  • Life — Seeking God’s direction for what comes next
  • Career — Seeking God’s direction in work and life choices
  • Relationships — Seeking God’s direction with the people in your life

What happens at COMPASS

  • Main Talks — Pastor Godfrey Hom proclaims the gospel and applies God’s Word to the questions shaping this generation
  • Conversations — Guided discussions press the teaching further into how to find God’s direction
  • Breakouts — Focused, interactive teaching on thinking biblically when making major life choices
  • Prayer — We ask God to strengthen faith and open spiritual eyes to see His leading
  • Worship — We sing spiritual songs together to worship our God and Navigator
  • Fellowship — Meet like-minded Christians rooted in a common faith in Jesus Christ

Code of Conduct

A Christ-Honoring Environment

Creating a Christ-honoring, respectful, and secure environment for every attendee.

1. Zero-Tolerance Anti-Harassment Policy

Treat every attendee, speaker, volunteer, and hotel staff member with Christian love, dignity, and respect.

2. Same-Gender Hotel Roommate Policy

All attendees sharing a hotel room must be of the same gender, unless sharing exclusively with verified immediate family members (spouse, parent, sibling). Mixed-gender room sharing outside of immediate family is strictly prohibited.

3. Respect for Venue & Boundaries

Honor quiet hours, hotel property rules, and private spaces. Uninvited entry into another attendee’s hotel room or unauthorized guests at the conference is not permitted.

4. Alcohol-Free & Substance-Free Environment

To maintain a safe & welcoming atmosphere, alcohol & illegal substances are prohibited.

5. Strict Enforcement & No-Refund Policy

Any individual found violating this code of conduct will be immediately expelled from the conference with no refund.
